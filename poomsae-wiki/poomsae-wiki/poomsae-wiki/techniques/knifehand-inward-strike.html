Knifehand Inward Strike

Tips

  • The hand blade, used in the move, extends from the wrist to the first joint of the little finger
  • First open your hand, place the four fingers from the index to the little finger together
  • Then bend the tip of the thumb and place it on top of the first joint of the index finger
  • And bend the tips a little inward
  • Place the wrist of the thrusting arm at shoulder level, and the supporting arm at the solar plexus level
  • Perform the Sonal Anchigi move by pulling both elbows towards your body
  • Place your supporting hand back at the wrist
  • The wrist of the striking hand should be straight
  • And your hand blade should be raised near your neck level
  • These are the four key moves of Taeguk Samjang.
