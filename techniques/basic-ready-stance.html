Basic Ready Stance

stance

Gibon Junbi Jase기본 준비자세

Speed:

From Taegeuk Il Jang at 2:41

Tips

  • Gibon Junbijase begins with Moaseogi, where you stand and move your left foot about a foot's length to your left
  • Breathe in, open your fists and raise them from the lower abdomen up to the solar plexus
  • Breathe out, then lower your fists back to the lower abdomen
  • Your face, torso, and lower body should be balanced along the center line
  • Your weight should be placed evenly on both feet
  • Both elbows should be bent like the internal angles of a hexagram
  • Your fists should be placed apart about a fist's length
  • Retain 30% of your breath in the lower abdomen once the preparatory move is complete
  • Be careful not to raise or tense your shoulders

Used in

Taegeuk Il Jang
Sources: Kukkiwon / World Taekwondo Academy · WT Poomsae Competition Rules (Sept 2024)
