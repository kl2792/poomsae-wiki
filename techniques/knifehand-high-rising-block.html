Knifehand High Rising Block

Tips

  • Cross the arms so the blocking arm is outside to block the face
  • The hand of the blocking arm rises first
  • Raise the hand first at face elevation and snap the elbow upward to block
  • Pull the opposite fist to the back
  • Cross the hand blade with the center line
  • The space between the blocking arm and forehead should be about one fist
  • Tilt the arm so that the fist faces upward and the elbow faces downward
  • Be careful not to let the elbow of the blocking arm open backwards as this will weaken the arm
